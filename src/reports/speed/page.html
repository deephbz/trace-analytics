How to read the time budget
Routes from fastest to slowest end-to-end speed. Each bar is the wall time of a call, request to end of response, split into its phases in time order and summed over the route's calls. TTFT floor: TTFT the context explains. TTFT variance: TTFT above the floor. Decoding: streaming time. Stall during decoding: pauses of 500 ms or more after the first token, which include hidden reasoning. Stall after last token: time from the last streamed update to the end of the response.

Population, time zones, and metrics
Each cell is one UTC hour of one UTC day: the median over its calls. Bands mark UTC hours named by the region whose working day dominates them. Right: each day's medians per region band, so a trend across days shows apart from the hour of day. Bottom: each hour pooled over all days, p50 line and p25–p75 band. Relative speed divides a call's end-to-end speed by its route's p50, so pooled routes do not show a route-mix shift; it does not control context or output length. TTFT variance is TTFT above the route's context floor. Cells under 5 calls and day-band points under 20 calls are left out.
