Where call time goes

Routes from fastest to slowest end-to-end speed. Each bar is the wall time of a call, request to end of response, split into its phases in time order and summed over the route's calls. TTFT floor: TTFT the context explains. TTFT variance: TTFT above the floor. Decoding: streaming time. Stall during decoding: pauses of 500 ms or more after the first token, which include hidden reasoning. Stall after last token: time from the last streamed update to the end of the response.
