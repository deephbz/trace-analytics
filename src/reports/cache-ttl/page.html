Assumptions
  • Scope: Anthropic and OpenAI model families, whatever route served them, at public API prices. Other models are excluded.
  • A turn reuses the prompt of its lineage predecessor: the nearest earlier assistant request on its parentId path, on the same model, with no compaction between.
  • Observed cache reads decide the outcome where they cover the prefix, or where the gap was within the TTL the turn actually ran under. Only misses that expiry explains are replayed.
  • Routes that log no cache writes (openai-codex, magpie) have their uncached input priced as writes at the route's actual policy. These writes are estimated.
  • Every turn refreshes its entry. Cross-session shared prefixes, minimum cacheable length, and retention beyond the nominal TTL are not modeled; the last one biases against long TTLs.
  • Scenarios are compared with each other. Replay and actual cost differ by the cache reads that happened after the nominal TTL.
